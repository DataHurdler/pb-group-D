# End-of-day feedback form

Ready to use as is. Collected at 15:50, in the room, before people leave. Paper
or an online form; keep it to one side of one page. Anonymous.

---

**[Course title] · [Date]**

Circle one number for each: 1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

8. Which part of the day was **most** useful? Why?

10. What's one thing you'd change about the course?

11. Would you recommend it to a colleague? ☐ Yes ☐ Maybe ☐ No

12. Anything else?

---

## Reading the results

- Items 1 and 6 are the ones to watch: they ask whether people learned and
  whether they'll use it. Low scores there matter more than a low score on pace.
- Compare item 3 with item 7. If people mostly said "about right" but item 3 is
  low, the pace was uneven: some parts too fast, some too slow. The open
  answers will say which.
- Group the answers to 8 and 9 by module. A module that shows up in both is
  divisive: probably right for some learners and wrong for others.
