# Course Charter — AI and LLM Prompt Engineering

The fixed facts of this course. Everything not stated here is a design decision.

## Description (approved, word for word)

The quality of results produced by generative AI often depends on the quality of
the instructions it receives. Effective prompt design helps users communicate
their intent clearly, provide appropriate context, establish constraints, and
guide large language models (LLMs) toward more useful outputs.

In this hands-on course, participants will learn practical techniques for
designing, testing, and refining prompts for workplace applications.
Participants will explore how prompt structure influences AI-generated responses
and how thoughtful prompt design can improve quality, consistency, and
efficiency.

## Learning objectives (approved, word for word)

1. Explain how large language models use prompts and context to generate responses.
2. Write clear, structured prompts that produce more useful and relevant outputs.
3. Apply prompting techniques to improve accuracy, consistency, format, and usefulness.
4. Refine prompts through iterative testing and evaluation.
5. Use prompting strategies that improve efficiency while maintaining output quality.

## Audience

Working professionals in mixed roles. No programming is assumed or taught.

## Fixed constraints

- **One day, in person**: about six hours of class, plus lunch and two short breaks.
- **Very hands-on.** Lectures are short; learners spend most of the day working.
- **Learners' tools:** their own laptop and a **free** ChatGPT account. No paid
  features, no file uploads, nothing to install.
- **Group work:** two groups of learners. Group prompts are run by the instructor
  on Claude and shown on the projector.
- **No confidential data** in any prompt: no student, personnel, or health
  records.

## Out of scope (other courses in the series cover these)

- Introduction to AI and Machine Learning
- Building an AI Agent
- AI Ethics and Governance
- Data Analytics, Cleaning and Visualization in Excel

## What a learner leaves with

A tested prompt template for one recurring task from their own job, that a
colleague could use without help.
