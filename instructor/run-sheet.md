## After the day

- Collect post-checks, feedback forms, and photos of flipcharts and the
  parking lot.
- Photograph or collect capstone deliverables if you said you would.
- Reset the room as agreed with the venue.
- Write three lines of notes before you leave: what ran long, what worked, and
  what to change. Add them to the retrospective (see
  [prep-checklist.md](prep-checklist.md#d--1-week-improve)).

## Keeping attention

The deck is short by design, for learners who find long presentations hard to
follow. A few habits make the most of it:

- **Keep a countdown timer on screen** during every activity, and leave the
  activity slide up until the timer ends.
- **Give one step at a time** for multi-step activities (1.2, 2.1), calling the
  next step when the timer runs out, rather than reading all the steps up front.
- **Repeat instructions in one sentence** after the slide goes up. Many people
  only start listening once they've found the slide.
- **Allow movement.** Standing, stretching, and fidgets are fine at any time; say
  so at 9:00.
- **Don't skip breaks** to make up time. Use the trim list in schedule.md.

## Contingencies

| If… | Then |
| --- | --- |
| Projector fails | Send the slide PDF by email or chat so learners follow on their laptops; use the whiteboard for demos. |
| Wi-Fi fails | Everything needed is on the USB stick. Have learners copy it around. Phone hotspot for the instructor. |
| Gemini is down or blocked on the venue network | Switch Gemini demos to the expected outputs in reference-solutions.md; learners pair up on phones (Gemini mobile app or browser on mobile data). |
| A learner's setup won't work | Pair them with a neighbour for the activities. Don't spend lecture time debugging. Fix it at a break or with the co-facilitator. |
| Running 10+ minutes late at a recovery point | Apply cuts from [the trim table](../schedule.md#if-the-day-runs-long), in order. Tell the room the new time for the next break. |
| An activity is clearly too hard | Stop at the checkpoint, demo the first step on screen, and restart. Note it for the retrospective. |
| An activity is clearly too easy | Move to the extension. Use the spare minutes for parking-lot questions. |
| One person dominates discussion | "Let's hear from someone who hasn't spoken yet." Switch to pair discussion before share-outs. |
| A question would derail the block | "Great question, and I want to give it proper time." Write it on the parking lot and answer it in 05. |
| Far fewer learners than expected | Merge table groups so each has at least 3. Share-outs can include everyone. |
| You lose your voice or fall ill | Co-facilitator takes the hands-on blocks; slides have speaker notes so someone else could present. |
