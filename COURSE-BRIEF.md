# Course brief

**Prompt Engineering Essentials: Getting Reliable Results from Google Gemini**

A one-day, hands-on workshop that teaches working professionals with little or
no AI experience how to write prompts that get useful, trustworthy results from
Google Gemini, the AI tool available to this audience, and how to use it
responsibly at work.

Every module, the syllabus, the agenda, and the slides follow from this page.

## 2. Design choices

- **Built for Google Gemini.** Every demo, exercise, and screenshot cue uses
  Gemini, because it's the tool this audience has. The course teaches the
  Gemini features that matter for reliable, responsible use: starting a new chat,
  the Sources button, the difference between work or school and personal
  accounts, and saving a reusable prompt as a Gemini skill. The prompting skills
  themselves transfer to any AI tool. Nothing needs a paid plan.
- **Designed for limited attention spans.** Some learners find long or dense
  presentations hard to follow, including people with ADHD or other attention
  difficulties. The deck is cut to 43 slides with one idea per slide; every
  activity slide has the same structure (numbered steps, a visible time, a
  "done when" line); section openers show where the day has got to; no talk
  segment runs past 12 minutes; and the day mixes in movement (the energizer,
  the gallery walk). These choices help everyone, not only learners who
  disclose a need.
- **Fictional data only.** Every case, memo, and name in the materials is
  invented. This keeps the day safe for professionals in regulated fields and
  shows the data-safety habit taught in module 03.
- **One running case.** A fictional company, Prairie Point Logistics, appears
  in the demos, the memo exercise, and the afternoon case, so learners don't
  have to learn a new scenario each time.
- **A framework that's easy to remember.** CRAFT: Context, Role, Ask, Format,
  Tone & audience. It's taught in module 01 and used all day.
- **Learners leave with something they'll use.** The capstone is a tested,
  reusable prompt template for a task from their own job.

## 4. Audience

- **Who:** working professionals from any field (business, education,
  healthcare, government, nonprofit) who have access to Gemini and want to use
  it in their daily work
- **Assumed:** everyday computer and web skills; writing emails, reports, or
  other work documents
- **Not assumed:** any AI or programming experience, or technical vocabulary
- **Why they come:** professional development. Most have tried Gemini or a similar tool and
  been underwhelmed, or haven't started because they don't know where to begin.
- **Likely worries:** "Will this replace my job?", "Is it safe to use?", "I'm
  not technical." Module 00 and the limits segment in 01 address these
  directly.

## 5. Scope

- **In scope:** what LLMs are and aren't; the elements of an effective prompt;
  examples, grounding, structure, and iteration; checking output; data privacy
  and responsible use; reusable prompt templates
- **Out of scope**, with somewhere to point people who ask:
  - Building AI applications, APIs, or coding with AI → developer documentation
    in [participants/resources.md](participants/resources.md)
  - Image, audio, and video generation → mention only
  - Choosing an AI vendor for an organization → their IT or procurement team
  - Legal and policy questions about AI in a specific workplace → their
    organization's AI policy and compliance office
