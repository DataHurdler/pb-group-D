## Segment notes

**Review (15:30).** Read out the themes from the morning flipchart. For each
one, ask the room: "can you do this now?" Then go through the parking lot. Any
question you can't answer in a sentence goes into the follow-up email; say so.
Common parked questions in this course, and short answers:

- *Will AI take my job?* It changes tasks more than it removes jobs. The
  people who benefit are the ones who know how to direct it and check it, which
  is what today was about.
- *Which tool is best?* The one your organization approves. Everything today
  works in all the major tools.
- *Can I trust it for facts?* Only what you can verify, or what it took from
  source text you gave it.

**Post-check (15:37).** Same questions, same check code as the morning. Say "this
shows what today changed, and it tells me what to improve."

**Action plan (15:44).** In the workbook, with three prompts. Suggest
learners write down the specific task their capstone template covers, and a day
they'll first use it:

1. *One thing I'll do with this in the next week:*
2. *What might get in the way:*
3. *Who or what will help:*

Learners keep it. If the follow-up email will include a reminder, ask them to
photograph it or write their email on a copy for you.

**Feedback (15:50).** Done in the room, before anyone leaves. Feedback collected
afterwards by email gets a much lower response rate. Leave the link or form on
screen until 16:00.

**Close (15:55).** Point to the participant resources
([participants/resources.md](../../participants/resources.md)), say when the
follow-up email will arrive and what's in it, and thank people. Finish on time.

## If running late

Shorten the review to the parking lot only. Never cut the post-check or
feedback form. If you must, give the action plan as something to take home.
