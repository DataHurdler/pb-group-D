## Segment notes

**Welcome (9:00).** Start on time, even if people are still arriving. Late
arrivals pick up logistics from the slide. Say the finish time out loud: people
relax when they know when they'll be done.

**Pair introductions (9:05).** Prompt on screen: *your name, what you do, and
one thing you'd like AI to help you with at work.* Expect a mix of
enthusiasm and scepticism, and welcome both: "a healthy dose of scepticism is
exactly the right attitude, and by this afternoon you'll know when to trust it
and when not to." Two minutes each way.
Then ask 3–4 volunteers to share their partner's "one thing". Write the
recurring themes on the flipchart and refer back to them at 15:30.

**Pre-check (9:13).** Say clearly that it's *not graded* and that blanks are
fine. The point is to measure what today changes. People write their check code
(see the assessment file) so pre and post can be matched without names.

**Outcomes and agreements (9:20).** Show the learning outcomes from
[COURSE-BRIEF.md](../../COURSE-BRIEF.md) as plain statements. Suggested working
agreements:

- Questions any time. If one would take us off track, it goes on the parking
  lot and gets answered by the end of the day.
- In hands-on time, try for two minutes before asking for help, then ask.
- Laptops open for activities, lids half-down during short lectures.
- **Fictional data only today.** Nothing personal, confidential, or from a real
  client goes into an AI tool during the course. Say why: it's the first
  habit of responsible AI use, and module 03 comes back to it.

**Setup check (9:25).** Everyone opens their AI tool and the workbook
([materials/handouts/workbook.md](../../materials/handouts/workbook.md)) and
types: *In two sentences, explain what you can help me with at work.* Seeing a
response proves the account, the browser, and the Wi-Fi all work. Anyone stuck
raises a hand. Fix quick problems now; for anything slower, pair the person with
a neighbour (or lend them the instructor's backup account) for module 01, and
fix it at the 10:30 break.

## If running late

Shorten introductions to one line each, in table groups (saves 4 minutes). Never
skip the pre-check.
