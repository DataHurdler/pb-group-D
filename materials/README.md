## Fill in before use

Placeholders are highlighted yellow in the Word files and marked `[TODO]` in the
Markdown. They are all logistics: date, room, instructor, contact details,
unit, lunch, and whether a certificate is offered.

## Deck structure

| Slides | Module | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–8 | 00 Welcome | Title, logistics, introductions, pre-check, outcomes, agenda, agreements, setup check |
| 9–20 | 01 Foundations | How LLMs work, four limits, CRAFT, activities 1.1 and 1.2, exit question |
| 21–33 | 02 Core skills | Show and ground, two live demos, activities 2.1 and 2.2, exit question |
| 34–41 | 03 Applied practice | Energizer, data traffic light, verify, hybrid pilot case, gallery walk |
| 42–46 | 04 Capstone | Brief, template skeleton, rubric, share-outs |
| 47–52 | 05 Wrap-up | Summary, post-check, action plan, feedback, close |

White slides are for teaching, black slides are for activities and section
openers, and every activity slide shows its time in the top-right corner. Prompts
always appear on a dark card in monospace, so learners can tell at a glance what
to type.

## Printing

- **Workbook:** one per learner, double-sided. Also share it digitally: learners
  copy the source texts into their AI tool.
- **Agenda:** one per learner, or attach it to the pre-course email.
- **Hint cards** for activity 3.1: from
  [modules/03-applied-practice/README.md](../modules/03-applied-practice/README.md),
  cut up, 2–3 sets.
- **Pre/post check:** two per learner, or an online form, from
  [assessment/pre-post-check.md](../assessment/pre-post-check.md).
- **Feedback form:** one per learner, from
  [assessment/feedback-form.md](../assessment/feedback-form.md).

## Accessibility

- Slide body text is at least 14pt, with high contrast; colour is never the only
  signal (the traffic light also uses words).
- Share the workbook and slides digitally for screen readers or enlarging.
